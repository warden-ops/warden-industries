Frequently asked questions

Your questions, our answers.

Why YubiKeys?
Yubico is a Swedish and American company, of allied origin. Its keys support FIDO2 and WebAuthn, which protects against credential phishing. We can also work with other keys that meet these standards.
What if a key is lost?
Each person receives a backup key or a recovery method planned in advance, and the lost key is revoked.
Who sells and invoices the services?
Warden Industries sells, delivers and invoices the services. Every engagement starts with a written quotation and a contract.
How do you handle our confidential information?
Information gathered during an engagement is only seen by the people who need it. The terms are set out in the contract, including a confidentiality agreement on request.
