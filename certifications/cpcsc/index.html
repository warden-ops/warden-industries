CPCSC

Qualify before the contract requires it.

The CPCSC sets cybersecurity requirements for companies working with Canadian defence. It rests on the ITSP.10.171 standard, aligned with NIST SP 800-171. The requirement is checked at contract award.

What we do

  • Gap assessment against ITSP.10.171
  • Prioritized remediation plan
  • Level 1 self-assessment support
  • Level 2 assessment preparation
  • Documentation and evidence
  • Annual follow-up

The framework at a glance

The program levels.

  • Level 113 ITSP.10.171 controls, annual self-assessment. Required at award in select defence contracts since summer 2026
  • Level 298 controls, third-party assessment by SCC-accredited bodies. Select contracts from spring 2027
  • Level 3Assessed by National Defence
  • StandardITSP.10.171, aligned with NIST SP 800-171

Who certifies

What Warden does, and what belongs to the assessor.

At Level 1, your own organization performs and signs its self-assessment. At Level 2, the assessment is done by a third party accredited by the Standards Council of Canada. Level 3 is assessed by National Defence. Warden prepares and supports, without assessing or certifying.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, our answers.

Does the CPCSC apply to us?
It depends on your defence contracts and the level they require. Show us the clauses and we will tell you what applies and when.
Can Warden perform our Level 2 assessment?
No. Level 2 is assessed by a third party accredited by the Standards Council of Canada. Warden prepares your organization for that assessment.
Can we start with Level 1?
Yes. Level 1 is an annual self-assessment. We help you complete it and, if useful, prepare what comes next.
Who sells and invoices the services?
Warden Industries sells, delivers and invoices the services. Every engagement starts with a written quotation and a contract.
How do you handle our confidential information?
Information gathered during an engagement is only seen by the people who need it. The terms are set out in the contract, including a confidentiality agreement on request.
