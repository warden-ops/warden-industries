CyberSecure Canada

A trust signal for your customers and partners.

CyberSecure Canada is a voluntary federal program for small and medium-sized organizations. It rests on 13 baseline controls from the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, verified by an accredited certification body.

What we prepare

  • Incident response plan
  • Automatic patching and secure configuration
  • Strong authentication and access control
  • Encrypted backups
  • Employee awareness
  • Network perimeter, cloud and website security

The framework at a glance

Program requirements.

  • ProgramFederal, voluntary
  • TargetSmall and medium-sized organizations
  • Basis13 baseline controls
  • AssessmentAudit by an SCC-accredited body
  • MarkValid for two years

Who certifies

What Warden does, and what belongs to the assessor.

CyberSecure Canada certification is issued after an audit by a certification body accredited by the Standards Council of Canada, under the Government of Canada program. Warden does not certify and does not conduct this audit.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, our answers.

Can Warden certify us?
No. Certification is issued after an audit by an accredited body. Warden prepares your organization and your evidence.
Who can aim for CyberSecure Canada?
The program is aimed at small and medium-sized organizations. The exact conditions are published by the program, and we confirm them with you at scoping.

How do you handle our confidential information?
