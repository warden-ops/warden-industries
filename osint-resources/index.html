A curated OSINT toolkit for operational security teams.

Open source intelligence (OSINT) means working with publicly available information. At Warden Industries we use it to see an organization the way an outsider would: exposed domains and services, leaked credentials, the digital footprint of teams, suppliers and partners.

This is a short selection, chosen for what is useful in operational security. Links open in a new tab.
