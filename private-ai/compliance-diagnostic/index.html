Frequently asked questions

Your questions, our answers.

Does Warden Industries provide legal advice?
No. We provide technology, configuration and documentation tools. Have the compliance documents reviewed by your own legal counsel.
Where is our data processed?
On hardware installed on your premises, or in colocation in Montréal for the Enterprise package. The firewall refuses outbound traffic by default.
Which models do you use?
Gemma 4 (Google), Ministral 3 (Mistral AI) and gpt-oss (OpenAI), under Apache 2.0 licences. Models from China-based labs are excluded.
Who sells and invoices the system?
Warden Industries sells, installs and invoices the system. Every engagement starts with a written quotation and a contract.
Do our drawings and processes leave the site?
No. The models run on your hardware and the firewall refuses outbound traffic by default, which helps you meet the confidentiality agreements you have with clients and partners.
How many people can use it at the same time?
The number of simultaneous users is confirmed during sizing, based on the hardware chosen.

Not legal advice

Warden Industries provides technology, configuration and documentation tools. We do not provide legal advice. The compliance documents we prepare with you are working documents: have them reviewed by your own legal counsel, and refer to the guidance of the Commission d’accès à l’information.
